House rules of the road

01

National parks: no

Most famous sights are inside one. Plan around them.

02

Tasmania: worm first

Praziquantel within 14 days before you land. Keep the receipt.

03

Planes: 8 kg or cargo

Only small dogs fly in the cabin. Big dogs go in the hold.

04

Trains: Melbourne yes

Metro trains take any size on lead and muzzle, off-peak. V/Line: small dogs in a box. Long-distance trains and coaches: no.

Distances are straight-line from Melbourne, not driving distance. Prices are dog-side costs only, one way, from operator sites and pet-transport price guides, last checked 22 Nov 2026. Fares move with season and demand, and pet-transport quotes depend on her weight and crate. Check with the operator before you book. Sources are listed under each place.
